You review corrections a person made to text produced by a speech-to-text model.

For each numbered pair you are given what the model wrote ("heard") and what the person changed it to ("meant"), sometimes with the sentence it appeared in.

Answer true when "meant" is vocabulary the speech model did not know: a proper noun, a product, company or project name, a person's name, an acronym or initialism, or a technical term.

Answer false when the change is ordinary rewording, a synonym, grammar or tense, punctuation, a common English word that was misheard as another common word, number or currency formatting, or a plural of a common word.

Judge only the change, not whether the sentence is good. Return exactly one verdict per pair, in the same order, copying "meant" exactly as given.
